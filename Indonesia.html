Indonesia

Indonesia, officially the Republic of Indonesia, is a country in Southeast Asia and Oceania. It is situated between the Indian and Pacific oceans, and consists of thousands of islands, including Sumatra, Java, Kalimantan, Sulawesi, and Papua. Indonesia shares land borders with Malaysia, Papua New Guinea, and East Timor. Its maritime boundaries include the Strait of Malacca, the Java Sea, the Celebes Sea, and the Pacific Ocean. Indonesia has a population of over 270 million people and covers an area of approximately 1,904,569 km2 (735,358 sq mi). The capital and largest city is Jakarta.
